Dental implants

Book an implant consultation

Three parts, one new tooth.

An implant replaces the whole tooth, not only the part you can see.

  1. Titanium implantA small screw placed in the jawbone under local anaesthetic. Over 3 to 6 months the bone bonds with it, and it becomes the new root.
  2. AbutmentA connector screwed onto the implant once it has healed. It holds the crown.
  3. Porcelain crownThe visible tooth, made to match the shape and shade of your own teeth.

How treatment works

Most of the time is healing: the bone needs 3 to 6 months to bond with the implant.

  1. Consultation and 3D CT scanDr Tom Ellis checks the bone and plans the position. £150, deducted if you go ahead.
  2. Placing the implantUnder local anaesthetic, usually about one hour for a single implant.
  3. HealingThe bone bonds with the titanium over 3 to 6 months. You can have a temporary tooth meanwhile.
  4. Abutment and crownThe abutment and your final porcelain crown are fitted.

Is it right for me?

Not everyone is suitable, for example if there is not enough bone. The consultation and CT scan show what is possible.
