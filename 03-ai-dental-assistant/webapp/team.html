The team

Three dentists, a hygienist and an orthodontic therapist on Upper Street. Between us we speak English, Hindi, Urdu, Polish and Ukrainian.

SK

Principal dentist

General and cosmetic dentistry, 15 years in practice. Sarah sees new patients for examinations, check-ups, fillings, whitening, bonding and veneers.

TE

Implants and oral surgery

Tom places and restores all our implants, from the CT scan consultation to the final crown, and carries out surgical extractions, including wisdom teeth.

PN

Priya leads our children's dentistry and specialises in nervous patients. Tell us when you book and she'll plan a longer appointment.
