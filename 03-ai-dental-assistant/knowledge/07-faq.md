# Frequently asked questions

## Do you offer NHS treatment?
No. BrightSmile Dental is a private practice. For NHS dentists near you, use the NHS "Find a dentist" service.

## How long is the wait for a new patient appointment?
Usually within 1 week. Emergency appointments are usually the same or next day.

## Do you do Saturday appointments?
Yes, Saturday 9:00 to 15:00.

## Can I get a free consultation?
Clear aligner and whitening consultations are free (30 minutes). Other consultations are charged as a new patient examination (£75).

## Is teeth whitening safe?
Yes, when done by a dentist with professional products after an examination. Whitening is not suitable for everyone, so we check your teeth first.

## Do fillings hurt?
We numb the area first, so most patients feel only a small scratch from the injection.

## Do you see children?
Yes, children of all ages. Dr Priya Nair leads our children's dentistry.

## Can I bring my own X-rays?
Yes, please bring or email them before your first visit.

## Is there Wi-Fi in the waiting room?
Yes, free Wi-Fi is available.
