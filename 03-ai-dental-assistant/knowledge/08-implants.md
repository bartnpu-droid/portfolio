# Dental implants

## What a dental implant is
- A dental implant replaces a missing tooth from the root up. It has three parts:
- The implant: a small titanium screw placed in the jawbone. It acts as the new root.
- The abutment: a connector screwed onto the implant. It holds the crown.
- The crown: the visible porcelain tooth, made to match the shape and shade of your teeth.

## How treatment works at BrightSmile
1. Consultation with a 3D CT scan (£150, deducted if you go ahead). Dr Tom Ellis checks the bone and plans the position.
2. Implant placement under local anaesthetic, usually about one hour for a single implant.
3. Healing: the bone bonds with the titanium. This usually takes 3 to 6 months. You can have a temporary tooth meanwhile.
4. The abutment and the final porcelain crown are fitted.

## Is it right for me?
Not everyone is suitable, for example if there is not enough bone. The consultation and CT scan tell us what is possible. Our team will explain the options, including bridges and dentures.
