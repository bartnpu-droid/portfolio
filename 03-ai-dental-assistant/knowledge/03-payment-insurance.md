# Payment, finance and insurance

## How to pay
- We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay and cash.
- Payment is taken at the end of each appointment.
- For treatment plans over £1,000 we take a 20% deposit when you book.

## Interest-free finance
- 0% finance over 6 or 12 months for treatment plans from £500 to £15,000.
- Longer terms (up to 36 months) are available with interest. Subject to status.

## Insurance
- We are a private practice and do not offer NHS treatment.
- We work with Bupa, AXA Health, Vitality, Simplyhealth and Denplan. We give you an itemised invoice so you can claim back from your insurer.
- We cannot bill insurers directly; you pay us and claim back.

## Membership plan
- BrightSmile Care Plan: £19.50 per month. Includes 2 check-ups and 2 hygiene visits a year, 10% off treatment and worldwide dental emergency cover.

## Cancellations
- Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or move an appointment.
- Missed appointments or late cancellations are charged £40 (£80 for hygiene, surgical or implant appointments).
- Deposits are refundable if you cancel more than 48 hours before the appointment.
