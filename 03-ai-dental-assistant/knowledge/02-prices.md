# Treatments and prices

All prices are private fees in GBP. You get a written treatment plan with the full cost before any treatment starts.

## Check-ups and hygiene
- New patient examination (includes 2 X-rays): £75, 40 minutes
- Routine check-up (existing patients): £55, 20 minutes
- Child check-up (under 16): £35
- Hygiene appointment (scale and polish): £85, 40 minutes
- Airflow stain removal: £95
- Check-up + hygiene combined: £125

## Emergency
- Emergency appointment (same day where possible): £95, includes examination and pain relief
- Out-of-hours emergency (registered patients): £150

## Fillings and restorative
- White (composite) filling: from £140 to £240 depending on size
- Root canal treatment, front tooth: £450
- Root canal treatment, premolar: £550
- Root canal treatment, molar: £750
- Porcelain crown: £750
- Simple extraction: £150
- Surgical extraction (including wisdom teeth): from £300

## Cosmetic
- Teeth whitening, home kit with custom trays: £350
- In-surgery whitening (Boost) + home kit: £595
- Composite bonding: from £250 per tooth
- Porcelain veneer: £950 per tooth

## Orthodontics
- Clear aligners (Invisalign-style), mild cases: from £2,500
- Clear aligners, comprehensive cases: from £3,950
- Free 30-minute aligner consultation

## Implants
- Implant consultation with CT scan: £150 (deducted if you go ahead)
- Single dental implant with crown: from £2,500

## Children
- Children under 16 of registered parents get check-ups at £35 and fissure sealants at £30 per tooth.
