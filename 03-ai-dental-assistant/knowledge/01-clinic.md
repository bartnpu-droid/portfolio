# About BrightSmile Dental

BrightSmile Dental is a private family dental clinic in Islington, London (fictional demo clinic).
We look after adults and children, and we see nervous patients every day.

## Address and getting here
- Address: 18 Upper Street, London N1 0PQ
- Nearest Tube: Angel (Northern line), 4 minutes on foot
- Buses: 4, 19, 30, 38, 43, 56, 73, 341 stop outside
- Parking: no private parking. Pay-and-display bays on nearby side streets; Islington Green car park is 3 minutes away.
- Step-free access: ground-floor surgery and accessible toilet.

## Opening hours
- Monday to Friday: 8:00 to 19:00
- Saturday: 9:00 to 15:00
- Sunday and bank holidays: closed
- Last appointment starts 30 minutes before closing.

## Our team
- Dr Sarah Khan, Principal Dentist: general and cosmetic dentistry, 15 years in practice. Sees new patients for examinations, check-ups, fillings, whitening, bonding and veneers.
- Dr Tom Ellis, Dentist: implants and oral surgery. Places and restores all our implants, and carries out surgical extractions, including wisdom teeth.
- Dr Priya Nair, Dentist: children and nervous patients. Leads our children's dentistry.
- Emma Clarke, Dental Hygienist: hygiene appointments (scale and polish) and keeping gums healthy between check-ups.
- Lucy Grant, Orthodontic Therapist: looks after our clear aligner patients.
- You can ask to see a particular dentist when you book.
