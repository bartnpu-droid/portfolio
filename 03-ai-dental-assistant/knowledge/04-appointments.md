# Booking and appointments

## How to book
- Ask the assistant to request an appointment: give your name, phone number, the treatment and the days and times that suit you. Our reception team will call or text you to confirm, usually within 2 working hours.
- Or call us on 020 0000 0000 during opening hours.
- Or use the booking form on our website (the "Book a visit" page).

## New patients
- We are accepting new adult and child patients.
- Please arrive 10 minutes early for your first visit to fill in a medical history form, or complete it online from the link we text you.
- Bring a list of any medicines you take and, if you have them, previous X-rays.

## Nervous patients
- Tell us when you book. We give longer appointments, explain every step and you can raise a hand to stop at any time.
- Dr Priya Nair specialises in nervous patients.
- We offer oral sedation for suitable adult patients on assessment.

## Children
- Children can come from their first tooth. We recommend a first visit by age one.
- A parent or guardian must attend with children under 16.

## Running late
- If you are more than 15 minutes late we may need to rebook so we do not delay other patients.
