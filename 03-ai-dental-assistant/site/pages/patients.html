New patients

We're accepting new adult and child patients. A new patient appointment is usually available within a week, and emergency appointments the same or next day.

  • Arrive 10 minutes early to fill in a medical history form, or complete it online from the link we text you.
  • Bring a list of any medicines you take and, if you have them, previous X-rays. You can also email X-rays before your visit.
  • A new patient examination costs £75, takes 40 minutes and includes 2 X-rays.

Nervous patients

Tell us when you book. We give longer appointments, explain every step and you can raise a hand to stop at any time. Dr Priya Nair specialises in nervous patients, and we offer oral sedation for suitable adult patients after an assessment.

Children

Children can come from their first tooth, and we recommend a first visit by age one. A parent or guardian must come with children under 16. Children of registered parents have check-ups for £35 and fissure sealants for £30 per tooth.

Payment, finance and insurance

We are a private practice and don't offer NHS treatment. You get a written plan with the full cost before treatment starts.

Paying

  • Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay, Google Pay and cash.
  • Payment is taken at the end of each appointment.
  • For treatment plans over £1,000 we take a 20% deposit when you book.

Interest-free finance

0% finance over 6 or 12 months for treatment plans from £500 to £15,000. Longer terms, up to 36 months, are available with interest. Subject to status.

Insurance

We work with Bupa, AXA Health, Vitality, Simplyhealth and Denplan. You pay us and we give you an itemised invoice to claim back from your insurer. We can't bill insurers directly.

BrightSmile Care Plan

£19.50 a month. Includes 2 check-ups and 2 hygiene visits a year, 10% off treatment and worldwide dental emergency cover.

Cancellations and late arrivals

  • Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or move an appointment.
  • Missed appointments and late cancellations are charged £40, or £80 for hygiene, surgical and implant appointments.
  • Deposits are refunded if you cancel more than 48 hours before the appointment.
  • If you're more than 15 minutes late we may need to rebook, so other patients aren't delayed.

Aftercare

General guidance our dentists give after routine treatment. If you're worried, contact the clinic.

After a filling
  • If you had a numbing injection, avoid hot drinks and chewing until the numbness wears off, usually 2 to 4 hours, so you don't bite your cheek or tongue.
  • Mild sensitivity to hot and cold for a few days is common.
After an extraction
  • Don't rinse, spit forcefully, smoke or drink alcohol for 24 hours.
  • Avoid hot drinks and strenuous exercise for the rest of the day.
  • If it bleeds, bite firmly on a clean, damp gauze or handkerchief for 20 minutes.
  • From the next day, rinse gently with warm salty water after meals for a week.
  • Contact us if bleeding doesn't stop, or if pain gets worse after 2 to 3 days.
After teeth whitening
  • Some sensitivity for a few days is normal.
  • Avoid coffee, tea, red wine and smoking for 48 hours for the best result.
After a hygiene appointment

Gums may feel tender and bleed a little for a day or two. Keep brushing and cleaning between your teeth.

Emergencies

Call 999 or go to A&E if you have

  • Swelling that affects your eye or spreads to your neck
  • Difficulty breathing or swallowing
  • Heavy bleeding that won't stop
  • A serious injury to the face, mouth or teeth

Severe toothache, a broken tooth or swelling of the gum

  • During opening hours, call 020 0000 0000 and ask for an emergency appointment. We keep slots free every day.
  • Registered patients out of hours: call our emergency line, 07000 000000.
  • Not registered and out of hours: call NHS 111 for urgent dental advice.

Knocked-out adult tooth

  • Hold it by the crown, the white part, not the root.
  • If it's dirty, rinse it briefly in milk or saline. Don't scrub it.
  • If you can, push it back into the socket and bite on a clean cloth. Otherwise keep it in milk.
  • See a dentist within one hour.

Questions

Do you offer NHS treatment?

No. BrightSmile Dental is a private practice. To find an NHS dentist near you, use the NHS "Find a dentist" service.

Can I get a free consultation?

Clear aligner and whitening consultations are free and take 30 minutes. Other consultations are charged as a new patient examination, £75.

Do you have Saturday appointments?

Yes, on Saturdays from 9:00 to 15:00.

Is teeth whitening safe?

Yes, when it's done by a dentist with professional products after an examination. Whitening isn't suitable for everyone, so we check your teeth first.

Do fillings hurt?

We numb the area first, so most patients feel only a small scratch from the injection.

Where can I park?

We don't have private parking. There are pay-and-display bays on nearby side streets, and Islington Green car park is 3 minutes away.

Is there Wi-Fi in the waiting room?

Yes, free Wi-Fi is available.
