Inside the ear cup

Materials

Made to be held.

Three materials, chosen for how they feel after a ten-hour flight, not how they look in a photo.

Aluminium

One piece of 6063 aluminium for each yoke. Cold to the touch for a second, then it simply disappears.

Protein leather

Soft, breathable and wipe-clean. The cushions replace in seconds, so the headphones outlive them.

Memory foam

Slow-recovery foam spreads the pressure across the whole ear cup instead of three points.

Questions

Good to know.

Do they fold for travel?

Yes. The ear cups turn flat and fold in, and the hard case is 4 cm thick.

Can I replace the battery?

Yes. It sits behind the right cushion, held by two screws. A new one costs €29.

Will they work with my phone and laptop at the same time?

Yes, they stay connected to two devices and switch to whichever one starts playing.

Can I use them with a cable?

Yes, the 3.5 mm cable is in the box, and it works even when the battery is empty.
