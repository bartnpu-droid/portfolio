About this piece
Burn and care

Burn for two hours the first time, so the wax melts to the edge. Trim the wick to 5 mm before every burn and keep it to four hours at a time. Stop when 1 cm of wax is left.

Shipping and returns

Ships in 1–2 working days in recyclable packaging. Unused items can be returned within 30 days.
